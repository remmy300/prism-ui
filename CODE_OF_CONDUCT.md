# Code of Conduct
We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Our pledge:** make participation harassment-free for everyone regardless of age, background, identity, experience, or appearance.

**Expected:** be kind and respectful, give and accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community.
**Unacceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, doxxing, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove contributions and ban participants who violate these rules. Report issues by opening a confidential request to the repository owner, @freizo, via GitHub.
